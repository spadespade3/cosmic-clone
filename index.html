04About

Why work with me?

Every page should have a job to do. I design the page and build it.

i.

Custom, not copied

No templates. Each project is designed around its purpose — the layout, copy, and structure follow the job the page needs to do, not a stock pattern.

ii.

Design + development

One person does both, end to end: designed, built, deployed. No throwaway mockups — what you approve is what gets shipped.

iii.

Direct communication

One person, one reply. You talk to the person doing the work — not an account manager relaying to a team. Straight answers, faster.

iv.

Honest scope

If your project needs a complex backend I can't deliver well, I'll tell you before we start — and point you somewhere that can.

Not sure if we're a fit? I'd rather say "this isn't my lane" up front than take your money and disappear. If I can't build it well, I'll point you somewhere that can.

This site is a working example of the approach: no frameworks, no build step, shipped as a handful of static files — responsive, keyboard-accessible, and fast to load.

06FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's included in a website?

A custom design, responsive implementation that works across modern devices, and deployment so it's live. The exact scope depends on the package you choose.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on the scope, but you'll get a realistic estimate after the brief. Landing pages and single-page sites move fastest.

How do revisions work?

Each package includes two revision rounds on the design. Revisions are feedback-based and handled in defined rounds. Additional rounds are treated as extra scope.

Do you provide hosting?

Deployment is available on the chosen package. The domain is not included. Hosting is set up and explained during the project; you'll own your site and content. Renewal costs are handled directly by you through the hosting provider.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. A redesign starts with reviewing what you have and works from there. It's a good fit for the custom project pricing.

Can I request a custom project?

Yes — if it fits the scope of what I build. Start a project and describe what you need; if it's not a fit, I'll tell you honestly.

How do I start?

Reach out on any channel in the contact section below — email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, or Viber. Tell me what you're building and you'll hear back with next steps and an estimated scope.
